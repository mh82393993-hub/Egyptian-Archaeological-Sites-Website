Giza

The Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx

The Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx

Egypt's and the world's greatest archaeological landmarks; they include the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, built as royal tombs in the Old Kingdom and guarded by the famous Sphinx.

View More👁

Saqqara and Dahshur

Saqqara and Dahshur

The cradle of pyramid architecture in ancient Egypt; Saqqara holds Djoser's Step Pyramid, the oldest stone building in history, while Dahshur is famous for the Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid of King Sneferu.

View More👁

The Grand Egyptian Museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum

The world's largest archaeological museum dedicated to a single civilization; located next to the Giza pyramids, it houses over 100,000 artifacts, most notably the complete Tutankhamun collection and the statue of Ramesses II.

View More👁

Luxor

Karnak Temple

Karnak Temple

The world's largest ancient religious complex, in Luxor; built over centuries to worship the god Amun, and famous for the Great Hypostyle Hall with its 134 giant columns and the Avenue of Rams.

View More👁

Valley of the Kings and Queens

Valley of the Kings and Queens

The final resting place of New Kingdom pharaohs and queens on Luxor's West Bank; its rock-cut tombs are decorated with vivid carvings, most notably the tombs of Tutankhamun and Queen Nefertari.

View More👁

Temple of Hatshepsut (Deir el-Bahari)

Temple of Hatshepsut (Deir el-Bahari)

A unique architectural masterpiece at Deir el-Bahari in Luxor; it has three tiered terraces carved into the heart of the rocky mountain, and its walls record Hatshepsut's famous expedition to the Land of Punt.

View More👁

Aswan

Abu Simbel Temples

Abu Simbel Temples

A rock-cut architectural wonder in southern Aswan; it contains the temples of Ramesses II and Queen Nefertari, and is famed for the sun aligning with the inner sanctuary twice a year.

View More👁

Philae Temple

Philae Temple

The pearl of the Nubian temples amid the Nile waters in Aswan; built to worship the goddess Isis, it features Ptolemaic and Roman reliefs and was moved entirely to Agilkia Island to save it from flooding.

View More👁

Cairo

Saladin Citadel

Saladin Citadel

One of the greatest medieval military fortresses, atop Mokattam Hill; built to protect Cairo, it contains notable landmarks, most famously the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, and offers panoramic views of the city.

View More👁

Old Cairo and Coptic Cairo

Old Cairo and Coptic Cairo

Home to one of the oldest and most famous historic churches in Old Cairo; it is called the Hanging Church because it was built over two towers of the Roman Babylon Fortress, and it is notable for its rare Coptic icons and unique style.

View More👁

Al-Azhar Mosque

Al-Azhar Mosque

A beacon of knowledge in the Islamic world and one of Cairo's oldest historic mosques; built in the Fatimid era, its architecture blends Fatimid, Mamluk and Ottoman styles.

View More👁

Alexandria and Other Governorates

The Roman Theatre in Alexandria

The Roman Theatre in Alexandria

One of the most prominent Roman landmarks, at Kom el-Dikka in Alexandria; it features tiered marble seating and, around it, the remains of Roman baths and ancient mosaics.

View More👁

Saint Catherine's Monastery in South Sinai

Saint Catherine's Monastery in South Sinai

One of the oldest working monasteries in the world, in South Sinai at the foot of Mount Moses; it is famous for its rare manuscripts, ancient icons and unique mountain setting.

View More👁

Temple of Amun in Siwa

Temple of Amun in Siwa

One of the best-known archaeological sites in the Siwa Oasis; linked to the worship of the god Amun and anciently famed for its oracle in the heart of the Western Desert.
